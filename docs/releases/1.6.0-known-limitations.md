# GizeMate 1.6.0: known limitations

Labels: REAL (observed by the builders), UNVERIFIED (not observed).

## Product
- One companion at a time. A saved profile ("take your companion to another device") still carries the cat only.
- A companion walking right at the left or right screen edge can have a few drawn pixels touching the window edge (cat 1 of 34 live samples, fox 2 of 36, slime 3 of 36). Probable cause: the shared roaming margin uses the cat's body width for every character. REAL observation, cause not isolated; a fix would change movement for all characters, so it is left as is.
- A paused companion is click-through (it cannot be picked up while paused).
- Turkish text has had no native-speaker review.
- The web and Android panel reloads the page when you switch character.
- Android notification text follows the device language, not the in-app language setting.
- macOS is not part of this release: its app bundle keeps the old name "Desktop Cat" and the macOS wording in Settings now says GizeMate.

## Distribution and signing
- **Windows installers are not code-signed** (no certificate exists). SmartScreen warns; Smart App Control may block them. The inner `DesktopCat.exe` is an unsigned Electron binary, as in earlier releases.
- Android: signed with the project's release key (the same one as the 1.5.0 build). Not distributed through Google Play; Play Protect may still ask for confirmation.
- Android updates over the 1.5.0 release-signed build in place. The earlier debug-signed "GizeCat (trial)" builds are a separate app and do not update in place.

## UNVERIFIED
- **The Windows installers were never executed.** Install, update over 1.5.0 and uninstall are reasoned from configuration and static inspection, not observed. Back up `%APPDATA%\Desktop Cat` before updating if you want certainty.
- **Windows on Arm:** packaged and statically inspected; never run on ARM hardware.
- **Android:** built and signature-verified only. No device, emulator or automated Android test was run by the builders; the owner tests on real devices.
- **Sound by ear:** the audio was analysed by measurement (levels, clipping, silence, repeats), but nobody has listened to it.
- Screen reader, high-contrast mode and real touch input were not exercised by a person.
- Multi-monitor, mixed DPI, Windows 10, multi-hour uptime and sleep/wake: not exercised (the soak is seven runs of 100 s accelerated, about 12 minutes).
- Downgrading to 1.5.0 after running 1.6.0.
