# GizeMate 1.6.0: known limitations

Labels: REAL (observed), UNVERIFIED (not observed).

## Product
- One companion at a time. A saved profile (move to another device) still carries the cat only.
- Characters walking near the screen's left or right edge can have a few drawn pixels touching the window edge (cat 1 of 34 samples, fox 2 of 36, slime 3 of 36 in the live test). The cause is the shared roaming margin (the cat's body width is used for every character); a fix changes movement for all characters and was left for an owner decision. REAL observation, cause is a hypothesis.
- A paused companion is click-through (cannot be picked up while paused).
- Turkish text has had no native-speaker review.
- The web and Android panel reloads the page when you switch character.
- Android notification text follows the device language, not the in-app setting.

## Distribution
- Windows installers are unsigned: SmartScreen warns and Smart App Control may block them.
- No Android 1.6.0 artifact was built (needs the owner's signing key; see the release checklist).
- macOS and iPad are not part of this release.

## Not verified (UNVERIFIED)
- The installers were never executed (install, upgrade over 1.5.0, uninstall are analysed from configuration only, not observed).
- Windows on Arm: packaged, never run on ARM hardware.
- Sound by ear: the audio was analysed offline (levels, clipping, silence) and WAVs were rendered, but nobody has listened.
- Screen reader, high contrast mode and real touch input: not exercised by a person.
- Multi-monitor, mixed DPI, Windows 10, hours-long uptime and sleep/wake: not exercised (the soak is 7 x 100 s accelerated, about 12 minutes).
- Downgrade to 1.5.0 after running 1.6.0.
